## The Dimension
Though the name will change in the future, the soul dimension holds a fallen kingdom. It was the place where a prosperous civilization once bloomed. This civilization, whose name is still unknown, had an economy mainly supported by blood extraction, blood exchange, and blood production. They developed technology around the idea of manipulating blood, while also worshipping their godlike blood idols.

## The Conflict
The boom of both technological and magical advancement propelled the civilization into a golden era of progress. However, this prosperity caused the population to spike faster than the rulers could handle. The demand for vital energy skyrocketed, and the natural production of blood could no longer sustain the sprawling metropolis. The kingdom was slowly bleeding dry, and this unmanageable growth soon pushed the economy to the brink of collapse.

## The Meeting
This uncontrollable population growth led their scientists and mages to explore alternatives for a more productive and energy-rich blood source. Months of intense investigation led to the discovery of ancient ruins holding twisted carvings of dark rituals and eldritch, god-like creatures. These beings were so entirely beyond human comprehension that their mere presence in depictions caused mind-shattering terror and profound confusion—a maddening revelation that would later be known as Insight.

Reaching the deepest rooms, they found a strange being, dated older than the creation of the kingdom itself. This creature took the shape of the kingdom's inhabitants to deceive them. It spoke their language and offered them salvation. Its words acted like honey, tempting them with every phrase it spoke. It taught the mages how to perform forbidden rituals and how to interact with those powerful beings from an otherworldly place without losing their minds.

## Back from Ashes
The rituals led to another exponential growth. At first, the deities acted generously, proposing a better future for the entire civilization. They offered alien flora that allowed the creation of new blood-like liquids. But it came at a heavy cost... The peasants' blood needed to be transformed and infected through a twisted process that often led to death. It was an infection, carefully controlled for the time being. The subjects who survived this transformation ended up as twisted, deformed beings with corrupted blood. This new liquid supplied by the dead subjects, mixed with the old traditional blood, allowed the development of an even better substance: the golden blood, the Rhnull.

The new deities also asked for tribute. They found the mortal blood supplied to them quite tasty in exchange for their strange liquids. These alien liquids were like nothing found in their home world—shimmering like gold. The new acquisitions allowed the kingdom to develop even stronger technology and powerful new magic that could even bring back the dead. It is still a mystery what these beings truly wanted, but the profits were undeniable.

## The Fall
Some researchers were still skeptical about these new eldritch rulers. The old blood gods were being forgotten, so these purists hid in underground facilities to find a better way of mixing the power of the old blood gods with the eldritch ones for a safer outcome.

Meanwhile, on the surface, everything started to crumble. The infection that once brought prosperity began to spread globally. Dark rituals were performed without constraints, and everything fell into chaos. People died in masses, and those who survived were corrupted forever.
